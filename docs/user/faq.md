# FAQ & Troubleshooting

## Account

**I did not get the verification email.**
Check spam, then request a new link on the sign-in page. Links expire after 24 hours.

**I am asked to accept updated terms.**
Our Terms, Acceptable Use Policy or Privacy Policy changed. Review and accept to continue.

**How do I delete my account or export my data?**
Email [support@secora.group](mailto:support@secora.group). See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## Scanning

**My scan says "authorization required".**
The target has no valid authorization or it expired. Open the target, confirm how you are authorized and set a new expiry date.

**My scan is refused: pending review or out of scope.**
Set the scope status to **In scope**, but only if your authorization covers that asset.

**My scan is refused for a private IP, localhost or government domain.**
The hosted service does not actively scan internal or certain government addresses. If you own the asset and have written authorization, contact [support@secora.group](mailto:support@secora.group).

**A recon job finished but one tool failed, or returned nothing.**
Open the job to see each tool's status. Other results are still saved. Some public sources are rate limited, so try again later.

## AI Copilot

**The copilot says no provider is configured.**
Add your own key under **Settings → AI providers**. See [AI Copilot](/docs/ai-copilot).

## Still stuck?

Email [support@secora.group](mailto:support@secora.group). To report a vulnerability in Secora itself, write to [security@secora.group](mailto:security@secora.group) (see [Security](/security)).
