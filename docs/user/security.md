# Security & Vulnerability Disclosure

We welcome reports of security vulnerabilities in PentestHub AI. The full policy is on the [Security page](/security).

## How to report

- Email **[security@secora.group](mailto:security@secora.group)**, or
- use **Report a vulnerability** on the project's GitHub repository (private advisory).

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Include the affected URL or component, steps to reproduce, the impact you observed and any proof of concept. Our machine-readable contact details are in [/.well-known/security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 5 business days.
- Updates as we investigate, and coordinated disclosure once a fix is available.
- Credit in the advisory if you want it.

## Testing rules for our platform

- Test only with your own accounts and data.
- No denial-of-service, spam or social engineering of our staff or users.
- Do not access, change or delete other users' data; stop and report as soon as you can show impact.

Reports made in good faith under these rules will not be pursued legally by us.
